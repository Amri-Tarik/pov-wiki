Builder¶

Profession · Timber

Areas and stages¶

Each area of the profession on the craft tree can be revealed once you have earned enough craft XP in the profession. Your profession stage is the highest stage among the areas you have reached.

Craft XP Stage Area Lore
122 1 1 Every beam has its place in a rising hall. A sure hand sets the timbers true and turns a humble shelter into a lasting home.
423 2 5 A long arm and a steady hammer reach the highest rafters. Hearths and sconces in a well-made hall burn their fuel slowly.
1,270 3 6 Tar-sealed beams and layered thatch keep the hall dry through storms. Deep joints stand against troll fists and the years; spare pegs never go to waste, and a well-tended bench serves the builder far beyond its walls.
2,126 4 2 Old builders could raise a longhouse from the timber another wasted on a shed. No peg or plank is squandered here.
3,726 5 4 Warm furs, carved pillars and a hearth at the centre. A hall built here welcomes the weary and lets them rest deeper.
4,969 6 3 The master builder walks the walls with a mallet, and cracks close wherever the mending reaches. Whole halls are made whole again in one round.

Quests¶

The game offers up to four quests per profession, drawn from these families. A family's variants add conditions or a target item. Targets, rewards and which variants appear depend on how many of the profession's areas you have revealed.

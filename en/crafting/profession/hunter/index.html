Hunter¶

Profession · Beast

Areas and stages¶

Each area of the profession on the craft tree can be revealed once you have earned enough craft XP in the profession. Your profession stage is the highest stage among the areas you have reached.

Craft XP Stage Area Lore
86 1 2 Feathers and flint by the fire. Sometimes the hunter finishes the evening with a few more arrows than were planned.
306 2 1 Arrows fletched quickly and quivers stuffed full. A busy hunter's camp turns out shafts by the bundle.
908 3 5 A patient hunter gathers a richer bounty from every hunt. Nothing useful is left behind in the grass.
1,535 4 3 A silent step carries farther than a hurried chase. The forest hears little as the hunter passes.
2,681 5 6 Deer fall in the meadows, and the hunter's pack is deep enough to carry them all. The forest provides generously here.
3,726 6 4 The hunter's quiver never seems to empty, and the kill yields more than expected. Ravens follow these trails eagerly.
4,969 7 7 A master hunter moves unseen and carries the weight of the kill as if it were nothing. The forest falls silent as the hunter passes.

Quests¶

The game offers up to four quests per profession, drawn from these families. A family's variants add conditions or a target item. Targets, rewards and which variants appear depend on how many of the profession's areas you have revealed.

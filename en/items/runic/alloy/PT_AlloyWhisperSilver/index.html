Whisper Silver¶

An unfinished incantation can be heard in the cooling metal.
Theme Sorcery
Effect Forging: sorcery properties far more likely
Potential 0
Azurite dust 8
Smelted from Whisper Silver Ore

All alloys: Alloys. How much more likely: how alloys weight the roll.

Properties it favours (181)¶

Forging with Whisper Silver makes these properties far more likely, wherever they can roll; on a given item only those that fit it are in the forge pool. A property that rolls on several kinds of item has a row for each.

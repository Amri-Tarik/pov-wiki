Map modifiers¶

A map packed in the Book of the Halls can be enchanted with runes for modifiers. Each modifier gives a reward, most of them with a danger attached. Higher-level maps roll stronger modifiers.

Use runes to enchant the halls with modifiers and increase rewards

Grades and runes¶

A map's grade follows the number of its modifiers; a map holds at most 6. A modifier family appears only once on a map, and some modifiers need a room type or a boss on the map.

Grade Modifiers
Normal map 0
Magic map 1–2
Rare map 3–6

Runes on a map¶

Runes are applied as on items: right-click the rune in the bag, then click the map in the book. They act only on a packed map before it is launched. A rune that can't act on the map, and any other rune, is refused and not spent.

Rune On a map
Rune of Spark Adds the first modifier to a normal map.
Rune of Weaving Adds a second modifier to a magic map with one.
Rune of Change Rerolls a magic map's modifiers into one or two new ones.
Great Rune of the Jarl Adds a modifier to a magic map.
Great Rune of Ascent Adds a modifier to a rare map, up to 6.
Great Rune of Oblivion Removes one random modifier.
Great Rune of Fate Rerolls the numbers of every modifier within its tier.

How a modifier rolls¶

A new modifier is picked by weight among those that fit the map. Its tier is then picked among the tiers the map's level reaches, by the tier weights below, and it gets a roll from 0 to 1: its numbers lie between the next tier's and its own tier's, by the roll (for the lowest tier, between 80% of its numbers and its numbers). Counts such as item racks or rune mines are rounded to whole numbers; stars and bosses take their tier's number.

Tier Map level Tier weight
T1 181 (Ashlands) 1
T2 97 (Plains) 2
T3 43 (Swamp) 3
T4 19 (Black Forest) 4
T5 0 (Meadows) 5

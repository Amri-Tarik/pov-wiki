Skill board¶

The skill board holds the skill gems in the equipment window, tab Skills. Gems work through the cells they share a side with.

Put a gem from the bag on an open cell: pick it up and click the cell, or right-click it in the bag. Gems work through the cells they share a side with: a trigger starts the effects next to it, supports change their neighbours, auras work by themselves once switched on. Drag gems between cells to swap them; right-click a gem to take it back.

Cells¶

The board has 19 hexagonal cells in two rings around the centre; 4 are open from the start (filled above). Each Awakening Chisel opens one more locked cell.

A Awakening Chisel opens it: right-click the chisel in the bag, then left-click this cell. An open cell adds no capacity.

How gems connect¶

  • Trigger: A gem that reacts to an event - a hit, a critical hit, a block, a parry, a kill - and starts every compatible effect next to it at once. Its effects make the attack or block that pays for them cost more stamina, whether the event happens or not.
  • Effect: A gem that does something when a trigger next to it starts it: a bolt, a wave, a heal, a curse. With two triggers beside it, it takes one of them; the board window switches which.
  • Curse: A debuff on an enemy: lower resistance, weaker blows, slower actions or more build-up taken. One player keeps one curse on each enemy; a repeated curse refreshes itself, a new one replaces the oldest.
  • Support: A gem that changes the effects and auras next to it, with its drawback. It never changes triggers or other supports, and it reaches nothing beyond its neighbours.
  • Aura: A gem that works by itself while switched on and reserves eitr. Supports next to it change it; the Circle of Gifts grants the aura forms of its supports, the Circle of Malice keeps its curses on enemies inside.

An effect takes one trigger beside it that can start it: the one chosen in the board window, or else the first in board order. A curse can take a Circle of Malice beside it instead. A trigger works only when it starts at least one effect; when it fires, it starts all of them. A support changes every effect or aura beside it whose tags it supports (listed on its page); the Circle of Gifts takes every support beside it. Auras need no trigger: they work while switched on.

Which trigger starts which effect¶

A trigger gives its effects what its event has: a living target, a shot, an incoming hit, a corpse. An effect that needs more than that stays unconnected.

States¶

A gem's tooltip on the board says what it is doing:

  • Working: connected and working.
  • Not connected: nothing beside it to work with.
  • Incompatible: only triggers that cannot start it are beside it.
  • Switched off: an aura that is switched off; left-click switches it on.
  • Waiting for eitr: an aura that does not fit in what is left of maximum eitr.
  • Over capacity: the board takes more capacity than it has: nothing on it works.

Capacity¶

capacity = 8 + min(12, ⌊passive points earned ÷ 10⌋)

Every gem on the board takes capacity, connected or not. A new character has 8; every 10 passive points earned add one, up to 20 at 120 points. Opening a cell adds none.

Gem Capacity
Trigger 2
Effect 3
Curse 3
Support 1 per supported gem
Aura 3
Aura: Circle of Gifts, Circle of Malice 4

Over capacity: the board does not work until gems leave it or capacity grows. Placing a gem that would take more capacity than the board has is refused.

Stamina cost¶

Hit, critical hit and kill triggers raise the stamina cost of every attack, hit or miss; block and parry triggers raise the stamina cost of every block, once per block. It is paid whether the trigger fires or not.

cost added by a trigger = Σ over its working effects (its price per effect + 3% × supports of that effect) × the effect's cost multiplier

The cost multiplier comes from the effect's supports: Long Seal makes it cost more, Thrifty Facet less; Long Seal's longer duration also adds half of itself on effects with the Duration tag. The attack's or block's stamina is multiplied by 1 + the sum of its triggers' costs.

Example: On Hit beside Eikthyr's Spark with two supports adds 6% + 2 × 3% = 12% to every attack's stamina.

Eitr reserve¶

A switched-on aura keeps part of maximum eitr for itself while it works; each support of the aura reserves more. If maximum eitr falls, the last auras in board order wait until it fits again.

reserve = (the aura's share × maximum eitr + 5 × supports beside it) × its supports' cost multiplier × (1 − reservation efficiency)

The share is of the whole maximum eitr. Auras reserve in board order out of what is left once your summons and bells hold theirs (held eitr): one that does not fit waits (Waiting for eitr) and the next one is still tried; with 1 maximum eitr or less, none fits. A switched-off aura, or a board over capacity, reserves nothing. Switching on or placing a gem that would leave an aura waiting is refused. Reservation efficiency counts up to 75%.

The Circle of Gifts adds its own amount per support instead; the Circle of Malice also adds 10 per curse it keeps, and 3–2.4 more for each Heavy Word on a kept curse.

Example: Warm Hearth (25%) with one support, at 200 maximum eitr, reserves 25% × 200 + 5 = 55 eitr.

Auras at work¶

aura strength = (1 + increased aura effect) × more aura effect × (1 + its supports' effect) × (1 + 10% × (rank − 1))

The strength multiplies the aura's own numbers (rune shield, regeneration, endurance, poison damage) and what the Circle of Gifts grants. Inner Circle adds to its supports' effect; Wide Echo takes from the effect of an aura that deals no damage.

An aura supported by Inner Circle affects only its bearer: allies around it gain nothing.

The Circle of Gifts grants each support beside it as an aura form (aura forms): its increases × the circle's strength × (1 + 10% × (support rank − 1)); its overflowing chances follow the support's rank alone, and its drawbacks soften with that rank.

Plague Breath and Answering Heat deal damage over time instead of hits. Every 0.5 s, Plague Breath deals 0.5 s of its poison damage per second to each enemy in range. Fire damage taken lights Answering Heat: for its duration, every 0.25 s it deals 0.25 s of its share of the fire taken in the last second. These ticks cannot be blocked or dodged, and neither stagger nor push; the character's increased and more damage count, with its damage for area, damage over time and auras (and spells, for a Spell gem).

Gems and your stats¶

A gem effect counts the character's own stats for what it is: increased damage and the damage of its kind (area, projectile, spell for a Spell gem), area of effect, effect duration, extra projectiles, pierce and chains, shot speed and knockback. Its supports' numbers (an overflowing chance, increased area, longer duration) add into the same sums as the character's own, and its hits then go through the character's hit rules like any other hit.

Curses¶

A debuff on an enemy: lower resistance, weaker blows, slower actions or more build-up taken. One player keeps one curse on each enemy; a repeated curse refreshes itself, a new one replaces the oldest.

One player keeps 1 curse on each enemy; Double Malice raises that (at most 8). Other players' curses do not count against yours.

A curse you already keep on the enemy refreshes: it lasts until the later end and keeps the stronger value. A new one past your limit replaces your oldest; a curse kept by the Circle of Malice is refused instead.

curse value = its value at its rank × (1 + increased curse effect + [Heavy Word](support-curse-power.md)), at most 75%

Passives that increase curse effect include Weight of Words.

duration = 6 s × (1 + increased curse and effect duration) × more effect duration × [Long Seal](duration.md) × [Heavy Word](support-curse-power.md), at most 120 s

Passives that increase curse duration include Weight of Words.

Ranks and cutting¶

A gem has a rank from 1 to 5. Every rank above 1 changes something:

  • What the gem does (damage shares, heals, curse values, support bonuses, an aura's whole effect) grows by 10% of its rank-1 value.
  • A drawback (a support's less damage, longer rest or higher cost; what an aura does to its own bearer) softens by 5% of its rank-1 value.
  • A support that adds projectiles, chains or pierces (Branching, Leap, Through the Ranks) gives an overflowing chance: +100% for each one it adds at rank 1, and +35% per rank above.
  • A trigger's chance below 100% grows by 10% of its rank-1 value, up to 100%.
  • A trigger that always fires (On Block, On Parry) rests 10% of its rank-1 cooldown less instead.
  • Areas, ranges, counts, limits, durations, the cooldowns of effects, costs, capacity and eitr reserve stay as they are, except where a gem makes one its benefit (Heavy Impulse, Rain Underfoot, Circle of Malice).
value at rank r = value at rank 1 × (1 + 10% × (r − 1))
drawback at rank r = drawback at rank 1 × (1 − 5% × (r − 1))
overflowing chance at rank r = 100% × added at rank 1 + 35% × (r − 1)
cooldown at rank r = cooldown at rank 1 × (1 − 10% × (r − 1)), for a trigger that always fires

An uncut gem's rank comes from the level of the creature or chest that drops it: 1 + ⌊level ÷ 15⌋, at most 5.

Rank Creature level From
1 0–14 Meadows
2 15–29 Meadows
3 30–44 Black Forest
4 45–59 Swamp
5 60+ Swamp

Right-click an uncut gem to open its cutting window: Uncut Trigger Gem cuts into any trigger, Uncut Effect Gem into any effect, curse or aura, Uncut Support Gem into any support. The gem gets the uncut gem's rank. The same uncut gem can instead raise one gem of its kind, in the bag or on the board, by one rank, up to its own rank.

Rules¶

  • Gems leave the board only out of combat.
  • One gem item goes on the board once; two gems of the same kind can both be there.
  • Right-click a gem in the bag to put it into the first free open cell.

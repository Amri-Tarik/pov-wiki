Evasion rating¶

Evasion rating gives a chance to avoid an attack or spell hit completely, capped at 150% before decay; each successful evasion multiplies the chance against every attacker by 80%, resetting after 8 without an eligible incoming hit. The cost ranges from 4% to 18% of maximum stamina according to the damage that would reach health after blocking, resistances, armour, endurance and rune shields, compared with maximum health; evasion cost reductions and a chance to spend no stamina apply. Damage-over-time ticks cannot be evaded, and a successful passive evasion triggers on-evade effects and interrupts stamina regeneration unless an effect prevents this; a manual dodge roll is a separate action.

How it works¶

Evasion rating gives a chance to avoid a hit, which depends on the attacker's accuracy (enemy accuracy), growing with its level and stars:

accuracy = (30 + 0.48 × level) × (1 + 15% × stars)
evade chance = evasion ÷ (3 × accuracy), at most 150%

Every evasion restarts your stamina regeneration delay, even one that costs no stamina; with Unbroken Footing, evasions leave stamina regeneration running.

An evaded hit applies nothing: no damage, ailments, status effects, stagger or knockback; nor does a hit you dodge.

An evasion costs stamina by how much of the hit would reach your health after all your defences (armour, resistances, glancing, rune shield, endurance): 4% of your maximum stamina for 30% of your maximum health or less, rising evenly to 18% at 2× your maximum health and beyond. Cost reductions for evading lower it, and a chance to evade without stamina makes it free; with less stamina than it costs, you can't evade.

Each hit is rolled on its own. Every hit you evade lowers your chance against every attacker: × 80% per evasion, until 8 s pass without a hit you could evade. A chance above 100% evades for sure until your evasions bring it under. Hits with no attacker (a spell whose caster is gone, say) are rolled against a level-1 creature's accuracy. Damage over time can't be evaded. Chance against creatures without stars, before any evasion, by biome:

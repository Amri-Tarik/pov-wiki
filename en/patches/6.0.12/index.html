Keywords¶

Changed (2)¶

Name Change
Area of effect Increases the size or reach of supported attacks and effects. It does not increase their damage; area damage is separate. An area never reaches through walls: an explosion, a ground slam, a burst or a chain hits only those with nothing solid between them and its centre.
Ascension A Lord's Brand ascends any piece of equipment to the biome after its boss. Weapons (pickaxes too), shields, armour and summoning staves: base damage (in the item's own damage types), durability, block and armour rise to that biome's items of the same kind and slot - nothing goes down; a summoning staff's minion becomes as strong as that biome's creatures. Their workbench upgrades start over at quality 1 and then cost what that biome's reference item costs. Properties, sockets, sigils and implicits stay. Equipment with nothing to compare - rings, belts, amulets, trinkets, utility items, capes without armour, other tools - gains only item level, unlocking higher property tiers on future rolls; its properties, implicit effects, base stats, durability and quality remain unchanged. One brand per item, and only upward.

New (1)¶

Name Change
Utility items The Wishbone, the Wisplight and the Dvergr lanterns are Magic at most: one prefix and one suffix, rolled from each item's own pool (its detection range, its mist-clearing area, its light and warmth, and a few common rows). They take no sockets and no quality: nothing on them scales with it. A Lord's Brand still raises their item level, which opens higher tiers for the next roll.

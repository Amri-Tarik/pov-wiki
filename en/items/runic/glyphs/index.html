Glyphs¶

Glyphs change the item or the rules of an action at the Runic Anvil (how it works). Valuable and very valuable glyphs also cost Divine Azurite Dust.

Where glyphs drop¶

Glyphs drop from chests, elite enemies and bosses; ordinary enemies drop none. Each kill or chest rolls once, at its source's chance; a hit gives one glyph, picked by weight among those its biome has opened. A glyph opens in its first biome and stays open after it. The biome is the one the loot's level belongs to; a boss without a level counts as its own biome.

Source Chance of a glyph
Boss 50%
Elite enemy, 2★ or more 12%
Elite enemy, 1★ 6%
Dungeon chest 10%
Chest above ground 3%

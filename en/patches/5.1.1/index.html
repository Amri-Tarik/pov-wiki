Changed (3)¶

Name Change
Dagger throw Replaces a one-handed knife's alternative attack ([Mouse 3]) with a throw. It remains a knife attack but uses ranged and throwing bonuses instead of melee bonuses; without weapon return, the knife stays where it lands.
Fortification A temporary, stackable reduction to damage taken from hits. Gaining a stack refreshes all stacks, subject to the gain cooldown and stack limit.
Each point of fortification makes hits deal 2% less damage to you. Passives of the tree give it on block, on hit, on stagger and the like - at most once every 1 s. It lasts 4 s, and every new gain restarts the timer of all points. The base limit is 5 points; passives can raise the limit and make each point stronger.

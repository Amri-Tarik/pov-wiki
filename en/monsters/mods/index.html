Monster Modifiers¶

Creatures with stars wear modifiers that change how they fight: faster, armoured, elemental, exploding on death and more. Their names show over the health bar, like "Swift Armored Greydwarf the Splitting".

Rarity¶

A creature's stars set its rarity, shown by the colour of its name and its glow. Each star brings modifiers; how many is a server setting.

Rarity Stars
Common 0
Magic 1–2
Rare 3–4
Unique 5+

Stars and modifiers also raise the quantity and rarity of what a creature drops.

How modifiers are picked¶

  • Every modifier is equally likely, and a creature never wears the same one twice.
  • A creature wears at most one element: Burning, Chilling, Shocking, Venomous.
  • Blink never rolls on creatures indoors (dungeons and other interiors).
  • Summoned bosses never roll these by default: Bound, Deathward, Glutton, Leaper, Magmatic, Mirror, Rootcaller, Splitting, Totemic, Unbreakable, Unseen.

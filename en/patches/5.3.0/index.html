New (8)¶

Name Change
Burning Ground Deals fire damage over time and builds burning through its explicit ailment channel. Spells create it when they hit terrain if you have the corresponding source. Identical overlapping ground does not stack.
Chilled Ground Deals cold damage over time and builds chill. All grounded characters slide on it, including its creator and allies. Sliding changes traction and braking, not movement speed. Identical overlapping ground does not stack.
Damage over Time Multiplier Applicable general and typed damage over time multiplier bonuses add together and form one multiplier. +20% general and +15% fire give fire damage over time ×1.35. This is separate from increased damage and does not repeat the original hit's damage modifiers.
Effective Range Bow and crossbow shots deal full damage only up to their effective range, a little beyond melee distance. Further away the damage falls off with the distance to the target, down to a fifth at five times that distance. Increased effective range moves both distances further out.
Ground Effects Ground effects occupy separate patches. Your base limit is 1 patch across all types; global skill limit and ground limit increase it. Each projectile can create a patch: three projectiles can leave three patches at limit 3. At the limit, a new patch replaces the oldest. Identical effects do not stack where they overlap. Ground damage is damage over time, not a hit or an aura.
Infusion Absorbing your ground with a staff shrinks the patch and accumulates matching infusion. The next eligible manually cast damaging spell consumes the stored infusion once. Its added damage is shared between the cast's projectiles and repeats; ordinary spell damage is not divided. Natural ground expiry grants no infusion. Absorption cannot repeatedly harvest overlapping area or recapture its own spent infusion for a net gain.
Poisoned Ground Deals poison damage over time and builds poisoning through its explicit ailment channel. Poison is its own damage type. Identical overlapping ground does not stack.
Shocked Ground Deals lightning damage over time and builds shock. Shock strength uses the source potency, independent of tick frequency. Identical overlapping ground does not stack.

War Steel¶

An alloy that knows both the blade and the weight of the hammer.
Theme War
Effect Forging: war properties far more likely
Potential 0
Azurite dust 5
Smelted from Scarlet Tin Ore

All alloys: Alloys. How much more likely: how alloys weight the roll.

Properties it favours (469)¶

Forging with War Steel makes these properties far more likely, wherever they can roll; on a given item only those that fit it are in the forge pool. A property that rolls on several kinds of item has a row for each.

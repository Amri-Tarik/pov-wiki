Pathfinder¶

Profession · Road

Areas and stages¶

Each area of the profession on the craft tree can be revealed once you have earned enough craft XP in the profession. Your profession stage is the highest stage among the areas you have reached.

Craft XP Stage Area Lore
55 1 1 A wayfarer's first lessons: leap without tiring, swim without drowning. Rivers and stones barely slow the path ahead.
184 2 2 A crackling campfire under the open sky. A wanderer finds true rest by its warmth, far from any hall.
547 3 8 Light boots and a long stride. Swift travellers cover the leagues between the meadows and the mountains before nightfall. Smoke rises from a ring of stones beside the trail. A seasoned traveller makes the wild a home with a single fire.
908 4 4 Stories by the fire and a pelt to sleep on. The road is long, but the wayfarer's camps grant a comfort like that of a mead hall.
2,215 6 3 A fall from a cliff is but a stumble to a seasoned wayfarer. And when death comes, the path back to the corpse is run with renewed vigour. Swift legs and a lofty leap carry the wayfarer over hill and stream. With each journey, the unknown map unfolds a little wider.
2,929 7 7 Old runes etched in stones and ruins call out from afar. Experienced travellers can sense their glow long before they reach them.
3,726 8 6 From every ridge, the wayfarer sees for miles. The fog of uncharted lands melts away before a wise traveller's gaze.
4,969 9 5 Even Valhalla's gate cannot hold back a true wayfarer. After death, they race back to reclaim their gear, swift and fearless.

Quests¶

The game offers up to four quests per profession, drawn from these families. A family's variants add conditions or a target item. Targets, rewards and which variants appear depend on how many of the profession's areas you have revealed.

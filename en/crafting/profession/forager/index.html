Forager¶

Profession · Mead and Herbs

Areas and stages¶

Each area of the profession on the craft tree can be revealed once you have earned enough craft XP in the profession. Your profession stage is the highest stage among the areas you have reached.

Craft XP Stage Area Lore
86 1 2 Raspberries in the meadows, mushrooms under the birches. A sharp-eyed forager often finds a second cluster hiding behind the first.
306 2 7 The forest feeds those who know where to look. Baskets fill with extra berries and woven bags hold more of nature's bounty. Wild greens grow back where they were picked, and the forager's hands snatch berries from further away. The woods are generous here.
908 3 4 In the shadow of old pines, mushrooms grow in thick rings. A forager who knows the spot comes back with twice the haul.
1,535 4 5 Picked thistle and bramble sprout anew with unusual haste. Old foragers knew the right way to leave a bush so it would bloom again.
2,681 5 6 A broad sweep of the hand gathers a whole patch at once. Berries and herbs from all around fall into the forager's basket.
3,726 6 1 Deep in the Black Forest, the ground is thick with plenty. Each bush seems to give a little more than it should.
4,969 7 3 Sturdy baskets and deep pouches. The master forager returns from the woods heavily laden with everything the land offers.

Quests¶

The game offers up to four quests per profession, drawn from these families. A family's variants add conditions or a target item. Targets, rewards and which variants appear depend on how many of the profession's areas you have revealed.

Woodcutter¶

Profession · Timber

Areas and stages¶

Each area of the profession on the craft tree can be revealed once you have earned enough craft XP in the profession. Your profession stage is the highest stage among the areas you have reached.

Craft XP Stage Area Lore

Lumberjacks here know how to make every blow and every fall count. A keen edge finds the perfect cut. Sometimes a single swing splits the trunk deeper than ten others could.

Trees fall faster and harder in this part of the forest. The axe finds the heartwood with a single, perfect blow. Lumberjacks here strike where the grain is weakest.

1,594 5 5 Every tree here gives more than its share. Logs pile up quickly, and the woodpile grows tall by evening.

4,969 9 2 At the edge of the world stands a forest of giants. A legendary lumberjack can shatter a whole tree with one strike and carry its timber home.

Quests¶

The game offers up to four quests per profession, drawn from these families. A family's variants add conditions or a target item. Targets, rewards and which variants appear depend on how many of the profession's areas you have revealed.

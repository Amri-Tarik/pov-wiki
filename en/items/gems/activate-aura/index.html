Circle of Gifts¶

Skill gem - Aura · Skill gems

Turns the supports next to it into their aura forms and grants them, with their drawbacks, to the bearer and allies within 8 m. Takes no effects or curses.

Property Value
Tags Aura, Area, Buff
Capacity 4
Eitr reserve 50%

While switched on it reserves 50% of maximum eitr, plus a fixed amount for each support beside it (eitr reserve).

Aura forms¶

Each support beside the circle is granted as its aura form, drawback included. Numbers from rank 1 to rank 5 of the support, for a circle of strength 1; the circle's strength multiplies the increases, not the overflowing chances or the drawbacks (auras at work).

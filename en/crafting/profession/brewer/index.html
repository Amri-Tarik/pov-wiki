Mead brewer¶

Profession · Mead and Herbs

Areas and stages¶

Each area of the profession on the craft tree can be revealed once you have earned enough craft XP in the profession. Your profession stage is the highest stage among the areas you have reached.

Craft XP Stage Area Lore
55 1 8 A carefully tended barrel gives a little more than expected. The first cellar rewards patience with a fuller horn.
184 2 1 Shelves of oak are built along the cellar wall for more barrels. Tar and oil simmer beside the mead, and they grow keener under a careful hand.
547 3 5 Racks line the dark cellar from floor to ceiling. A brewer who builds well finds room for one more cask, and then one more again.
908 4 4 Rendered fat and pine tar thicken in the brewer's pots. Oils made here burn hotter and keep their bite long after they are bottled.
2,215 6 7 Some batches seem to brew themselves, filling one more bottle than the recipe promised. Oil and mead alike spill over in plenty here.
2,929 7 3 The cellar answers a practiced hand. Mead matures swiftly, and the last measure sometimes fills another horn.
3,726 8 2 Deep in the cellar, oils age in sealed jars until they are dark as bog water. Their potency lasts long after the stopper is pulled. Old brewers whispered runes over each barrel. The mead from this cellar strikes harder, and its blessing lingers long after the horn is emptied.
4,969 9 6 The great vats of the mead hall reek of tar and resin. A master brewer coaxes an extra flask of oil from every pressing.

Quests¶

The game offers up to four quests per profession, drawn from these families. A family's variants add conditions or a target item. Targets, rewards and which variants appear depend on how many of the profession's areas you have revealed.

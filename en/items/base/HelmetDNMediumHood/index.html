Hood of the Vanguard¶

helmet · armour

Something to protect your neck from the elements as well as from the sharp teeth of the enemy.

Implicits¶

Always on the item. Ranges are rolled when the item is made.

  • (17-28)% increased draw speed

Forge of Potential¶

The idol that upgrades this item at the Forge of Potential: Bloodgold Protection Idol. It is not spent when crafting the item.

Item level¶

Crafted items are item level 246 (Deep North): this sets which property tiers they can roll.

Mods¶

Every property that can roll on this item, with values for this item's slot. The weight is a property's chance against the others of its type; the share is that chance among all of them here, whatever the item level. Higher item levels unlock more properties and better tiers.

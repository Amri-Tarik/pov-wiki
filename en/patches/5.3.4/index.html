Keywords¶

Changed (7)¶

Name Change
Ascension A boss essence ascends a weapon, shield or summoning staff to the biome after its boss. Base damage (in the item's own damage types), durability and block rise to that biome's weapons of the same kind - nothing goes down; a summoning staff's minion becomes as strong as that biome's creatures. Workbench upgrades start over at quality 1 and then cost what that biome's weapon costs. Properties, sockets, sigils and implicits stay. One essence per item, and only upward. Rings and belts can also ascend: only their item level rises, unlocking higher property tiers on future rolls. Their current properties, implicit effects, base stats and quality remain unchanged.
Crossbow reload Reload → Crossbow reload
Dexterity Per point: 0.185% increased stamina regeneration, 0.2% → 0.15% increased evasion rating. Dexterity also contributes to the skill multiplier of the weapons it governs.
Effective Range Bow and crossbow shots deal full damage only up to their effective range, a little beyond melee distance. Further → Beyond away the → it, damage first decreases gradually, then falls off → more with the distance to the target, down → steeply to a fifth at five → the times → far that distance → limit. Increased effective range moves → stretches both → the distances → entire further out → curve.
Evasion rating Evasion rating grants a chance to avoid a hit completely, including its damage, stagger, knockback and ailment. The chance weighs evasion rating against the attacker's accuracy and reaches 100% at 3 times the accuracy. Each evasion against the same attacker leaves 80% of the chance against it, until it lands no attack for 8 (30 for a boss). Each evasion costs 18% of maximum stamina; without that stamina the hit lands. Damage over time, persistent damaging ground and environmental damage cannot be evaded; a successful passive evasion triggers on evade, independently of a manual dodge roll. Passive evasions interrupt stamina regeneration unless an effect explicitly prevents this.
Evasion rating gives a chance to avoid an attack or spell hit completely, capped at 150% before decay; each successful evasion multiplies the chance against every attacker by 80%, resetting after 8 without an eligible incoming hit. The cost ranges from 4% to 18% of maximum stamina according to the damage that would reach health after blocking, resistances, armour, endurance and rune shields, compared with maximum health; evasion cost reductions and a chance to spend no stamina apply. Damage-over-time ticks cannot be evaded, and a successful passive evasion triggers on-evade effects and interrupts stamina regeneration unless an effect prevents this; a manual dodge roll is a separate action.
Fists Fist → Fists weapons → include and → both unarmed attacks share → and fist bonuses → weapons such as claws. Unarmed → Fist bonuses require → apply empty → to hands → both; unarmed attacks → bonuses count → apply as dual-wield → only without a shield and one-handed with a shield → weapon.
Slam An attack that hits the ground in an area and deals only blunt damage: the area attacks of sledges and similar weapons, and alternative attacks that a mastery turns into a slam. Slam bonuses and aftershocks apply to these hits. A slam is not a strike and never splits its damage between targets.
An area attack against the ground using blunt damage as its basis. Damage converted into blunt contributes and elemental conversion from that basis remains effective; raw non-blunt damage, slashing and piercing cannot be dealt by a slam. Sledges’ ground attacks and alternatives turned into slams gain slam bonuses and aftershocks, are not strikes and never split damage between targets.

New (3)¶

Name Change
Ability cooldown The waiting time before an ability can trigger again. It is separate from loading a crossbow magazine.
Armour effectiveness Armour effectiveness increases the armour value used to mitigate incoming hits without increasing the displayed armour rating. It does not increase armour-derived damage or other bonuses based on armour.
Unarmed Unarmed attacks use empty hands, without a fist weapon. They also count as fists; without a shield they count as dual-wield, and with a shield as one-handed.

Loot and experience¶

What a kill gives: Path of Valheiman loot, Valheim's own drops, and experience.

Numbers are the defaults of the Normal difficulty; servers can change them. Every formula here is checked against the mod's code with each patch.

Loot¶

When a creature dies, its Path of Valheiman loot is rolled with two numbers, item quantity and item rarity:

Quantity = 1 + item quantity + 0.1 × stars + 0.5 × modifiers
Rarity = item rarity + 0.2 × stars

Item quantity and item rarity are the killer's own, from passives and gear. Modifiers add quantity, not rarity. A kill by one of your summons, companions or pets counts as yours. The loot's item level is the creature's level; uniques and jewels keep it too (shown with Alt).

A creature, elite or boss killed in the Blight Mist also has a 0.2% chance to drop Womb of Corruption.

On a difficulty preset with more stars than Normal, creatures and elites (not bosses or chests) give less per star: the star and modifier terms of quantity and rarity are × the preset's star share, and their quantity × its quantity factor, so that loot per area stays near Normal's:

Preset Star share Quantity factor
Vanilla 100% 100%
Easy 100% 100%
Normal 100% 100%
Hard 68.12% 73.76%
Merciless 50.3% 55.48%

Drops per kill¶

Each kind rolls its own number of drops: on average this many × quantity.

Source Equipment Currency Jewels
Creature 1 in 77 1 in 67 1 in 846
Elite creature 1 in 14 1 in 22 1 in 107
Boss 1 in 3 1 in 7 1 in 16
Chest 1 in 4 1 in 17 1 in 31
Dungeon chest 1 in 3 1 in 14 1 in 36

By default, a piece of equipment only drops once you know its recipe (all its materials); currency, jewels and uniques have no such gate. Servers can switch each kind off per source (enemies, bosses, chests) and change their rates.

Rarity by item level¶

Gear never drops common: it is magic or rare, by these weights, which rarity shifts toward rare. Base jewels can also drop common, with a weight of 25% of the magic one (after rarity):

c = rarity ÷ (1 + rarity); magic × (1 + c), rare × (1 + 2c)
Level Common Magic Rare
1 90 9.5 0.5
19 78 19 3
43 62 29 9
77 54 33 13
97 50 34 16
130 45 36 19
181 40 37 23

Each gear drop has a 0.05% × (1 + c) chance to be a unique instead. Bosses always drop at least a magic item, a rare one from level 43.

Valheim drops¶

Valheim's own drops (meat, hides...) grow with stars; trophies and single items don't.

amount × (1 + 0.5 × stars), at most 100

Except for bosses, the star bonus is × the difficulty preset's star share.

Ascension¶

A boss essence ascends a weapon, a shield, a summoning staff, a ring or a belt to a later biome, once per item and only upward: the item's own biome (that of the highest-level material in its recipe; a staff's thrall's) must come before the essence's.

Essence Biome Level
Essence of Eikthyr Black Forest 19
Essence of the Elder Swamp 43
Essence of Bonemass Mountain 77
Essence of Moder Plains 97
Essence of Yagluth Mistlands 130
Essence of the Queen Ashlands 181
Essence of Fader Deep North 246
Essence of Kall Deep North 246

The item's base stats are compared with Valheim's own items of its kind from that biome (weapon kind and one- or two-handed, or kind of shield) and raised to their median; they never go down:

  • weapons: combat damage (everything but chopping and mining) rises to the highest median of that kind's weapons among the biomes from the item's own up to the essence's, keeping its damage types; so does damage per quality level. Kinds with no Valheim weapon of their own, elemental staves and sceptres rise by the most weapon damage grows from the item's biome to any biome up to the essence's, × the item's ascension damage multiplier from the server's item base rolls, if it has one.
  • Sceptre of the Pious: compared with all of Valheim's one-handed weapons instead, the same way; its damage per quality level becomes that of the essence's biome.
  • shields: block armour and block force, and their gain per quality level, become at least those of the biome's median shield of that kind.
  • summoning staves: the staff itself doesn't change, but its thralls are called as of the new biome (summon strength).
  • rings and belts: only their item level rises to the biome's; their quality and properties stay.
  • durability and its gain per quality level become at least the biome's.

The item goes back to quality 1, and upgrading it costs what the biome's reference item costs, at that item's station. Its item level rises to the biome's level. Properties, sockets and sigils stay. Corrupted and unidentified items can't ascend, nor pickaxes, bombs, traps, torches or weapons with less than 5 combat damage.

An upgraded item keeps at least the damage, block, parry force and durability it had at its quality.

Valheim's own summoning staves can ascend only while the server setting Vanilla summoning staves is off.

Dismantling into dust¶

Magic, rare, unique and legendary items can be dismantled into dust at the rune carver's table (this is not salvaging items, which returns recipe materials). Each property of the item is worth the azurite dust price of a rune, by its place among the item's properties; an item with no properties counts as one:

  • property 1: Rune of Spark, 15 azurite dust
  • property 2: Rune of Weaving, 20 azurite dust
  • property 3: Great Rune of the Jarl, 0 azurite dust
  • property 4 and every one after: Great Rune of Ascent, 0 azurite dust
dust = Σ rune price × tier multiplier; uniques and legendaries: dust × 40 × (1 + item level ÷ 100)
azurite dust = dust × 0.3, rounded, at least 1
uniques: divine dust = ⌊azurite dust × 0.1 ÷ 2⌋, at least 1; legendaries: ⌊azurite dust × 0.2 ÷ 2⌋, at least 1; the azurite dust then loses 2 for each divine dust

A property counts with its tier's multiplier; one without a tier counts ×1. A rune bought with other dust than azurite dust is worth nothing here.

Tier Multiplier
T0 ×2
T1 ×1.45
T2 ×1.2
T3 ×1
T4 ×0.85
T5 ×0.7

What an item gives with properties of tier T3 (×1); uniques and legendaries at item level 100 (azurite + divine dust):

Properties Magic or rare Unique Legendary
1 4 324 + 18 288 + 36
2 10 756 + 42 672 + 84
3 10 756 + 42 672 + 84
4 10 756 + 42 672 + 84
5 10 756 + 42 672 + 84
6 10 756 + 42 672 + 84

Unique jewels give dust by their item level instead: see Dismantling unique jewels.

Dismantling unique jewels¶

Dismantling a unique jewel gives divine dust and azurite dust by its item level:

divine dust = 40 × (1 + (item level − 1) ÷ 249); azurite dust = 10 × (1 + (item level − 1) ÷ 249); both rounded
Biome Level Divine dust Azurite dust
Meadows 1 40 10
Black Forest 19 43 11
Swamp 43 47 12
Mountain 77 52 13
Plains 97 55 14
Mistlands 130 61 15
Ashlands 181 69 17
Deep North 246 79 20

At the rune carver's table, each dismantle takes at most 0.5 s.

Data from Path of Valheiman 5.3.6, extracted 2026-10-27.

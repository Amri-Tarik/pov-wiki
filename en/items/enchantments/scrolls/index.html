Enchantment scrolls¶

Three kinds of scroll open enchantments. They are kept in the bag as currency. Right-click to learn: the scroll teaches its enchantments to the character that reads it and is used up. A scroll whose enchantments the character already knows is not spent. See all enchantments.

Scroll of Enchantment¶

Opens the enchantments of its theme. The scroll's stage (I to V) sets the highest tier it opens. Reading a higher stage also opens the tiers in between; a scroll of a stage you already have, or lower, teaches nothing.

Stage Opens
I Opens these enchantments from T5 to T5
II Opens these enchantments from T5 to T4
III Opens these enchantments from T5 to T3
IV Opens these enchantments from T5 to T2
V Opens these enchantments from T5 to T1

Where theme scrolls drop¶

Each loot drop from these sources has a chance to add a Scroll of Enchantment (when the server lets that source drop currency):

Source Chance
Boss 60%
Elite creature with 2 or more stars 8%
Elite creature with 1 star 4%
Dungeon chest 12%
Chest 3%

The scroll's stage follows the level of what it drops from: the stage of the biome that level belongs to. A boss without a level of its own counts by its biome, and the ocean counts as stage II.

Stage Biomes
I Meadows, Black Forest
II Swamp
III Mountain
IV Plains
V Mistlands, Ashlands, Deep North

The theme is picked at random among the themes that have enchantments and that the character hasn't yet opened up to that stage. When every theme is, no scroll drops: what you already know never drops.

Scroll of Ancient Will¶

Opens every enchantment of this boss: every tier of every enchantment of its boss.

When one of these bosses dies, every living player within 60 m of it gets a scroll of their own, unless they already know its enchantments. Only its owner can pick it up ("This scroll was left for another player").

Scroll of Exalted Enchantment¶

Opens the special T0 of this enchantment.

A boss above level 130 (the Mistlands' level; see creature level), in the world or in the halls, gives each living player within 60 m a chance of 8% at an exalted scroll of their own. It opens the T0 of a random enchantment that player doesn't know yet.

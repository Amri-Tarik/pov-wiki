Keywords¶

Changed (4)¶

Name Change
Crossbows A crossbow fires a loaded bolt without drawing. It must reload before the next shot.
Crossbows have a magazine: additional projectiles become loaded bolts, and additional projectile chance is rolled once when a reload finishes. Each shot fires one bolt; holding the attack keeps firing at the fire rate, based on the crossbow's own reload time. A saved bolt keeps both the ammunition and the loaded shot.
Projectile volleys Adds projectiles to a shot or throw. Extra arrows spread around the aim, while extra bolts fire in sequence; each consumes its own ammunition unless it is conserved.
Bows spread additional arrows around the aimed shot. Crossbows convert additional projectiles into loaded shots in their magazine. Pierce, chain and bounce continue each projectile independently; they do not spend another loaded shot.
Projectiles An attack travelling through the world before impact. Pierce passes through targets, chain seeks another enemy, and surface bounce reflects the projectile with reduced damage. Increased projectile damage adds to the same sum as every other damage increase. A bow or crossbow shot is two parts: projectile and arrow (or bolt) damage raise only the arrow or bolt, bow and crossbow damage only the weapon itself; everything else raises the whole shot.
Reload The time a crossbow takes to load the next bolt. Faster reload shortens this time without increasing shot damage.
Reload fills the magazine of Crossbows. Additional projectile chance rolls at successful reload completion. Reload speed shortens the reload; crossbow fire rate controls the interval between loaded shots. Cancelling a reload does not grant loaded shots.

New (5)¶

Name Change
Absorption With Gather the Elements and a staff in hand, standing on your own spell ground absorbs it: the patch visibly shrinks and its power goes into infusion. Absorption rate makes the patch shrink faster. Ground that simply expires gives nothing.
Ascension A boss essence ascends a weapon, shield or summoning staff to the biome after its boss. Base damage (in the item's own damage types), durability and block rise to that biome's weapons of the same kind - nothing goes down; a summoning staff's minion becomes as strong as that biome's creatures. Workbench upgrades start over at quality 1 and then cost what that biome's weapon costs. Properties, sockets, sigils and implicits stay. One essence per item, and only upward.
Crossbow fire rate Crossbow fire rate sets the time between loaded shots. Each crossbow's base interval is a share of its own base reload time, so a 3.5 s reload gives about one shot per second. Reload speed changes only the reload.
Loaded bolts Loaded bolts set how many shots one crossbow reload gives. Flat loaded bolts and additional projectiles add to the base of one bolt; increases and multipliers then apply to the whole sum. They never fire extra bolts per shot.
Magazine shots The first shot is the first bolt released after a completed reload. Saving that bolt does not renew the first-shot bonus. The last loaded shot is released with one loaded bolt remaining. These properties follow the bolt to impact; a one-bolt magazine has both. A dodge-triggered reload uses the normal reload, including costs, speed and instant-reload chance.

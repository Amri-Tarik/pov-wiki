Fisher¶

Profession · Table

Areas and stages¶

Each area of the profession on the craft tree can be revealed once you have earned enough craft XP in the profession. Your profession stage is the highest stage among the areas you have reached.

Craft XP Stage Area Lore
55 1 8 A quiet pier and a patient angler. The fish here seem to find the hook more readily, and the line reels in smoothly. Nets sometimes pull up more than herring. Old fishers swore the sea gives up lost treasures to those who wait long enough.
184 2 2 Hours at the water's edge with an easy cast. The seasoned angler wastes no breath fighting the line.
547 3 5 Swift hands at the reel bring the catch to shore before it can thrash free. The best fishers haul in fish before the tide turns.
908 4 3 Rich shoals crowd the quiet cove, and a patient angler brings home more than a single catch. Sometimes the deep tide offers stranger gifts: a barnacled chest or a lost amulet glinting beneath the fish.
2,215 6 1 A tight knot keeps the bait on the hook, and an easy cast spares the arm. The patient fisher loses little to the waves.
2,929 7 7 Here the water always seems alive with movement. Fish rise to the hook as if summoned by Njord himself.
3,726 8 6 A well-rested fisher has a calm mind and steady hands. After a night by the fire, the catch comes up in pairs.
4,969 9 4 Beyond the last skerry, the sea holds the wreckage of a thousand longships. Those who fish here pull up treasure as often as cod.

Quests¶

The game offers up to four quests per profession, drawn from these families. A family's variants add conditions or a target item. Targets, rewards and which variants appear depend on how many of the profession's areas you have revealed.

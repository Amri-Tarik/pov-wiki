On Hit¶

Skill gem - Trigger · Skill gems

When an attack hits an enemy: 25–35% chance to trigger the connected effects. Once per attack, at most every 0.6 s.

Property Value
Tags Attack
Capacity 2
Attack stamina cost +6% per effect

Each effect it starts: +6% attack stamina cost, paid on every attack, hit or miss

By rank¶

Its numbers at each rank (ranks).

Rank Effect
1 When an attack hits an enemy: 25% chance to trigger the connected effects. Once per attack, at most every 0.6 s.
2 When an attack hits an enemy: 27.5% chance to trigger the connected effects. Once per attack, at most every 0.6 s.
3 When an attack hits an enemy: 30% chance to trigger the connected effects. Once per attack, at most every 0.6 s.
4 When an attack hits an enemy: 32.5% chance to trigger the connected effects. Once per attack, at most every 0.6 s.
5 When an attack hits an enemy: 35% chance to trigger the connected effects. Once per attack, at most every 0.6 s.

On the board¶

Property Value
Starts Eikthyr's Spark, Night Brood, Frost Wave, Victor's Blood, Bowstring Echo, Ice Shards, Fire Burst, Flesh Rend, Broken Seals, Rotten Blood, Bared Soul, Broken Will, Norns' Fetters, Bared Steel, Trembling Bones, Shattered Mail
Cannot start Shard of Retribution (Needs an incoming hit: block or parry)
Cannot start Flame of the Fallen (Needs a kill: a kill trigger)
Cannot start Blood Legacy (Needs a bleeding kill)

Gems work only with the cells they share a side with (how gems connect).

How to get¶

Cut from an Uncut Trigger Gem.

A character carrying Sigil of the First Storm, Sigil of the Oath Devourer receives it once, at rank 3.

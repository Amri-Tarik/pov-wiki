# Path of Valheiman DB

A datamined reference for [Path of Valheiman](https://valheim.hexium.gg/mods/treextr/PathOfValheiman),
the Path of Exile–inspired passive tree, crafting and loot overhaul for Valheim.

Every page here is generated from the mod's own files. The text is produced by the game's own
code, so it matches what you see in game and updates with each patch.

## Passive tree

- [Keystones](passives/keystones.md): build-defining passives with a drawback
- [Notables](passives/notables.md): the larger passives, by category
- [Masteries](passives/masteries.md): passives where you choose one option

## Glossary

The [glossary](glossary/index.md) holds every term from the mod's in-game wiki. Underlined
keywords anywhere on the site link to their entry, and show its definition on hover.

!!! info "Unimplemented content"
    The game files contain content that isn't finished yet, like placeholder passives or stats
    without proper text. It is listed here too, marked **Unimplemented** or **Unfinished text**.
